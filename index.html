About Sidra Elite Platform

Sidra Elite Platform is a comprehensive solution combining a P2P marketplace (Eagle SDA Black Market) with a premium wallet node interface. We are not officially owned by Sidra Chain. We provide a non-custodial environment where buyers and sellers can exchange SDA for fiat with blockchain-backed security. Our escrow system ensures that SDA is locked in a vault until the seller confirms payment or a dispute is resolved.

The Wallet Node feature provides secure, non-custodial wallet services for sending and receiving SDA directly on the Sidra Chain.

User Guide & FAQ

How to Buy: Select an ad, enter quantity, and pay the NGN to the seller's bank. Click "I Have Paid" and wait for the release. SDA is held safely in escrow.

How to Sell: Deposit SDA to your Vault Address. Post an ad.

Safety Rule: You can only list 99% of your balance to ensure there is gas left to release the funds.

What is a Dispute? If a seller doesn't release the SDA after you have paid for more than 5 minutes, click Dispute. Admin will verify the payment and force the release if you provide payment proof.

Why can't I sell all my SDA? Because 1% is kept in your vault to cover the network fees (gas) required to send the SDA to the buyer.

What does the "Online" status dot mean? A green dot means the seller has been active within the last 60 seconds. Trade with "Online" sellers so that your SDA will be released much faster.

Wallet Node: Use the WALLET tab to send, receive, and manage your SDA. A network commission applies to transactions. Keep your mnemonic phrase safe — we cannot recover it.

Privacy Policy

Your security is our priority. Private keys for your vault and receiving wallet are generated locally in your browser and encrypted. We do not store your keys, PINs, or security answers on our servers. Loss of access credentials means loss of funds. Always keep your security answer safe. We do not track personal identity beyond your mobile number for account routing. You are responsible for backing up your security answer.

To ensure maximum privacy for the Wallet Node, we strictly avoid collecting Private Keys & Seed Phrases. We do not log your specific transaction history on our servers. Data lives on the blockchain. We do not store private keys. Support logs are encrypted.

Terms & Conditions

1. Commissions: Sellers are charged a 0.5% marketplace fee upon successful release.
2. Withdrawals: A 0.2% fee applies to transfers to external wallet addresses.
3. Compliance: Any attempt to spoof bank proofs or manipulate the system will result in an immediate BAN and vault LOCK.
4. The 99% Publishing Rule: To ensure network stability and prevent failed transactions, sellers are restricted from listing more than 99% of their current vault balance. This 1% reserve is maintained to cover blockchain gas fees.
5. Wallet Node: A network commission is applied to every wallet transaction. Users must not use this platform for money laundering or illegal activities. Blockchain transactions are irreversible. By using Sidra Elite, you agree that you are responsible for your own private keys. We cannot recover lost phrases.
